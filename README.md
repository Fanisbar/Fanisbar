# About Me:
Final-year student at the Department of Informatics and Telecommunications(DIT), National and Kapodistrian University of Athens (NKUA).<br>

I'm particularly interested in **Machine Learning** and enjoy building projects across **Deep Learning**, **Natural Language Processing**, **Computer Vision**, and **Data Mining**. My recent work includes deep learning experiments with **PyTorch**, NLP pipelines using **BERT/DistilBERT**, large-scale data mining and unsupervised learning, computer vision projects involving **CNNs and transfer learning** and algorithmic projects in C/C++ and Python.<br>
I'm particularly interested in understanding how algorithms work in practice — from implementing them from scratch to evaluating, optimizing, and visualizing their performance.<br>
